About Me

I am a law student at American University Washington College of Law, dedicated to justice. I extern with Montgomery County's Special Prosecution Division and will be a Rule 9 Legal Intern with Snohomish County Prosecutor’s Office. My research focuses on corporate accountability for war crimes, with published work on international criminal law. I supported peace negotiations with the Public International Law & Policy Group and studied international criminal law at The Hague's Asser Institute. I aim to pursue a career as a prosecutor in public service.
